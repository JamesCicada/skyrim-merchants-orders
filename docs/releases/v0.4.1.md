# Merchant Orders and Deliveries 0.4.1

- **Merchant-specific catalogs:** Orders now follow each merchant's current trade stock. Food vendors offer eligible food they carry, while blacksmiths and general merchants offer stocked common materials.
- Catalogs update with merchant stock and support properly configured food added by other mods.
- Orders remain limited to approved common materials and ordinary food/drink. Equipment, potions, poisons, rare materials, and known special consumables are excluded.
- Broader merchant support through vendor factions and trade chests, including standard innkeepers.
- Itemized quotes recheck availability and price before payment.
- Fixed the vendor detection flag that prevented Anoriath, Carlotta Valentia, Belethor, and other valid merchants from accepting orders.
- Existing paid orders keep their stored terms. Adjustable quantities and sourcing times, 5% courier delivery, and merchant pickup recovery remain available.

## Installation

Quit Skyrim and update both the core package and enhanced UI addon to 0.4.1. Dynamic catalogs require the enhanced addon, SKSE, SkyUI SE, and UIExtensions SE. Core-only installations retain the previous fixed material catalogs.

Reload your existing save; no quest reset or new save is needed. Do not mix the 0.4.1 addon with an older core.

Availability follows current stock, so sold-out goods may disappear until restocking. Additional materials require an allowlist patch.

## Validation

Nine scripts compile; plugin readback, archive integrity, record compatibility, and merchant detection regression checks pass. The detection regression covers Anoriath, Carlotta Valentia, Belethor, and Orgnar. In-game confirmation of the 0.4.1 repair is pending; this remains an early release.
